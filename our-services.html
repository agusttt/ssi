Our Services

Structural Design

Our Structural Design Services provide comprehensive solutions, including Code-Based Design for regulatory compliance, Performance-Based Design with advanced modeling for customized structural responses, and independent Design Peer Reviews to ensure efficiency and compliance. We also specialize in Seismic Design Solutions for new buildings, incorporating state-of-the-art techniques like base isolation and dampers to enhance resilience against seismic events, all while maintaining cost-efficiency and reliability.

Structural Assessment & Retrofitting Design

Our Seismic Assessment Services provide a range of solutions tailored to different project needs. These include Post-earthquake Assessments for quick on-site inspections to identify immediate risks, detailed evaluations in compliance with ASCE 41 guidelines, and Comprehensive Assessments that ensure resilience against future seismic events and recommend improvements to enhance performance. For SLF (Sertifikat Laik Fungsi), compliance with PP16 (Peraturan Pemerintah No. 16/2021) is ensured to meet regulatory standards. These services address both immediate safety concerns and long-term structural integrity, delivering actionable insights for effective risk mitigation.

Our Retrofitting Design Services focus on enhancing the seismic resilience and structural performance of buildings through carefully crafted solutions. To meet safety, durability, and compliance requirements, we provide strategies tailored to the building’s needs, incorporating techniques like base isolation, dampers, and structural strengthening where appropriate. These designs improve load capacity and stability while ensuring adaptability to modern regulations, offering long-term protection and enhanced performance for existing structures.

Value Engineering

Our Value Engineering service focuses on optimizing project outcomes by evaluating alternatives that reduce costs, improve performance, and enhance overall value without compromising quality or safety. We work collaboratively with clients to identify cost-effective solutions and innovative design improvements that align with project goals.

Structural BIM & Detailing

Our Structural BIM & Detailing Services utilize advanced BIM and CAD technologies to produce high-quality, coordinated construction documentation and precise structural detailing. We aim to deliver error-free structural drawings and shop details, enhancing communication, minimizing conflicts, and improving project efficiency. Additionally, we offer a separate Guidance Service to assist clients in the production of shop drawings, Bar Bending Schedules (BBS), and related documentation, ensuring that teams follow best practices and streamline workflows for optimal results.

Site Supervision

Our Site Supervision services ensures that construction projects are executed according to design specifications and regulatory requirements. Our experienced supervisors oversee day-to-day operations, monitor quality, and provide timely solutions to ensure smooth project progress and successful completion.

Concrete Technology Consulting

Our Concrete Technology Consulting services deliver tailored solutions to optimize concrete performance at every stage of your project. From mix design and testing to advanced construction techniques, we ensure durability, efficiency, and compliance with the latest standards. Whether you're utilizing high-strength, self-consolidating, or specialized concrete applications, our expertise ensures that your concrete meets the highest performance and safety standards.
